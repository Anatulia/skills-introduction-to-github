# Memex

A hypothetical device proposed by **Vannevar Bush** in the essay *"As We May
Think"* (The Atlantic, July 1945). It would store a person's books, records, and
communications and let them be linked by **associative trails** — chains of
connections between documents, where the links matter as much as the documents.
Widely seen as the intellectual ancestor of hypertext and personal knowledge
management.

## Why it's here
[[andrej-karpathy]] cites the Memex as the ancestor of the [[llm-wiki-pattern]].
Bush envisioned private, curated knowledge with associative trails, but —
per Karpathy — *"the part he couldn't solve was who does the maintenance. The
LLM handles that."* The LLM-Wiki is, in effect, a Memex with an automated
curator.
